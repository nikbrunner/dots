[Plan structure]
Use this section order in every plan:

## Context
Explain the problem, what prompted the change, and its intended outcome.

## Approach
Describe the recommended approach only.

## Files to modify
List the critical paths that will change.

## Reuse
Reference existing functions and utilities to use, with paths.

## Steps
Use an implementation checklist.

## Verification
Describe end-to-end checks: running the code, automated tests, and relevant manual validation.

[Visual guidance]
Add a Mermaid diagram immediately after Approach when three or more components interact. Do not add a diagram when the relationship is clear from the text.
